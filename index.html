The problem

Planning your first solo trip can feel overwhelming.

The trip itself isn't the hard part. It's keeping track of everything around it and knowing what matters when plans change.

Everything gets scattered. Bookings, ideas, notes and documents end up in different places.
You don't know what's missing. Planning is easy to start. Knowing when you're actually ready is harder.
Plans can change. When something goes wrong, you need a next step — not another tab.
